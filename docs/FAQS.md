> What about bvh-trees / octrees?

Sister libraries exist for those tree types (but maybe they have not been made public jet, ask me if you have any specific interest in what they can do).  
See [bvh3](https://github.com/karurochori/bvh3) and [oc3](https://github.com/karurochori/oc3).  

They will be shipped as separate libraries as their public interface cannot 100% overlap, but they share the same  overall structure and design principles.
