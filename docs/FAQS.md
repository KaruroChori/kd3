> What about bvh-trees / octrees?

Sister libraries exist for those tree types (but maybe they are not public jet, ask me if you have any specific interest).  
See [bvh3](https://github.com/karurochori/bvh3) and [oc3](https://github.com/karurochori/oc3).  

They will be shipped as separate libraries as they do not 100% overlap, but they share the same basics and I tried to keep their interface as close as reasonable.
