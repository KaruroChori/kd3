Comparison

Feature comparison based on kd3 1.5.0 and nanoflann 1.10.0. kd3 targets static low-dimensional clouds; nanoflann covers a broader feature set. Colors indicate advantage in this comparison, not simply whether a feature exists.

kd3 is stronger for

  • Static 2D-4D point clouds, especially near-data queries.
  • Volume and ray queries when subtree bounds are enabled.
  • First-class filtered 1-NN, k-NN, and distance queries.
  • Caller-owned k-NN buffers and a trivially copyable view.
  • C API, GLSL generator, and header-only C++23.

nanoflann is stronger for

  • Radius, approximate, and custom-metric search.
  • Dynamic, incremental, and runtime-dimensional indexes.
  • Non-owning adaptors and built-in index serialization.
  • C++11 compatibility and no OpenMP requirement.
  • A permissive BSD licence and a mature ecosystem.

Performance is configuration-dependent. kd3 wins clearly on near-data queries and on volume queries with AABB enabled; nanoflann remains the broader library. nanoflann's parallel build is present, but a global lock limits scalability. The benchmark page lists every measured row.
